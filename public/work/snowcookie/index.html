Snowcookie

A ski wearable that reads your body mechanics and measures fatigue, to prevent injuries on snow. I invented it and built it with Olympic champion Bode Miller.

Code on GitHub
1 / 5

What I built

Snowcookie is a small sensor pod for the skis and the body. It reads how you turn, how you load each ski, and how your form slips as you get tired, then shows it on your phone.

We took it from an idea to a product in shops, including Japan's biggest chain of sports stores. It was a finalist in Intel's Make It Wearable challenge.

How it works

  • PodsMotion sensors on each ski and on the body record every turn.
  • TurnsSoftware splits a run into turns and reads edge angle, speed and load for each one.
  • FatigueWhen your technique starts to slip late in the day, it tells you, before a tired turn becomes a fall.
  • AppYour runs and turns on the phone, in the lift queue.

What it taught me

Sensors on real bodies, in the cold, used by people who will never read a manual. Everything I build now starts from that, Lumos included.
