Clinical AI

Slumber AI

An assistant that answers sleep surgery patients' questions before their operation, in their own language.

slumber-ai.vercel.appCode on GitHub

What I built

Patients go into surgery with questions they forgot to ask, or felt silly asking. Slumber AI takes a short intake, then answers questions about that specific operation, in whatever language the patient writes in.

More than 40 sleep surgeons tested it, and I presented it with Robson Capasso at AAO-HNSF 2025.

How it works

  1. 01IntakeA short form tells it which operation the patient is having.
  2. 02AnswersEach question is answered from the material for that operation, in the patient's language.
  3. 03FeedbackWhen the patient is done, a short survey asks whether it helped.

What it taught me

Answer the question a person actually asked, in their words. Lumos does the same thing out loud.
