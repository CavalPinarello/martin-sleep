Sleep Performance

A live course that teaches people to read their own wearable, see what their sleep is doing to their days, and test a change on themselves.

Code on GitHub

What I built

Five lessons. You bring your own ring or watch, and by the end you know what your sleep is doing to your days, and you've tested one change on yourself, properly, over two weeks.

Every lesson opens with the verdict, and every claim carries a grade for how good the evidence is.

How it works

  1. 01MeasureYour own nights, from the device you already wear.
  2. 02UnderstandWhat the numbers mean, and which ones to ignore.
  3. 03TestOne change, two weeks, a clear answer.
