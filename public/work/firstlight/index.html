FirstLight

An iPhone and web app that measures how sharp you are each morning, against your own sleep.

firstlight-zoe-demo.vercel.appCode on GitHub
1 / 3

What I built

FirstLight starts the day with a 90-second check of how quick and steady you are. It reads the night from the ring or watch you already wear, keeps that data private, and learns your own pattern over a couple of weeks.

Then it lets you run small tests on yourself, like whether a 2 pm coffee costs you the next morning or whether creatine helps, and the answer is about you rather than an average person.

How it works

  • Morning checkShort tap and reaction tests, timed in milliseconds.
  • Your nightSleep data from your own wearable, kept on your side.
  • Your body clockA curve of when you're likely to be sharp today, from last night and your recent rhythm.
  • Small testsTwo-week experiments on one change, with the answer in plain words.
