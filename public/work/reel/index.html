Mac app

REEL

A Mac app that turns voice recordings and meetings into notes you can search, with the speakers named.

Code on GitHub
1 / 2

What I built

I record a lot: walks, calls, reading notes, ideas at 6 am. REEL pulls recordings off a pocket field recorder or the Mac's microphone, transcribes them, works out who spoke, and writes a short note with key points, action items and a mind map.

It learns voices from the corrections I make, and ideas and to-dos go straight to Tenzo. About 400 automated tests keep it honest.

How it works

  • CaptureA pocket recorder, the Mac's microphone, or a live call.
  • TranscribeSpeech to text, then each voice matched to a person.
  • NoteSummary, key points, action items and a mind map.
  • Hand-offIdeas and to-dos go to Tenzo.

What's next

A version that friends can try on their own Macs.
