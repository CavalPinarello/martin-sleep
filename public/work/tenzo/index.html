Personal AI

Tenzo

My AI chief of staff. It's named after the cook in a Zen monastery, the one who makes sure everyone else can do their work. It runs around the clock on a Mac mini at home and briefs me every morning.

Code on GitHub

What I built

Tenzo reads my calendar, mail, wearables and notes, and I talk to it in a chat. It writes the morning brief, keeps track of what I promised people, and answers questions about my own week.

It runs on a computer in my house rather than someone else's cloud, because it reads everything.

How it works

  1. 01ReadsCalendar, mail, last night's sleep, notes and voice memos.
  2. 02ThinksOn a Mac mini at home, around the clock.
  3. 03BriefsA short brief every morning, and answers in a chat whenever I ask.

What's next

An iPhone app, so the brief comes to the lock screen.
